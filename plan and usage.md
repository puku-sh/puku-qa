# **Plan & Usage** 

View your current Puku plan, monitor usage, and understand the limits associated with your account. 

The **Plan & Usage** page provides information about your active plan, usage limits, model usage, and available account features. 

You can access **Plan & Usage** from **Puku Settings** . 

## **Open Plan & Usage** 

### **1. Open Puku Settings** 

Open Puku Editor and select the **Settings** icon in the lower-left corner. 

<img width="975" height="517" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3d27873e-e5dc-4e4c-a53a-124f787cbc25" />



### **2. Select Plan & Usage** 

In **Puku Settings** , select **Plan & Usage** from the account section on the left.
